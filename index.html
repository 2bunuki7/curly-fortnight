About
me.

Finding the interesting parts
of unfamiliar things.

I'm Buna, an IT student from Prishtina, Kosovo. I like understanding how things work — not just how they look.

I spend much of my time learning software, trying out programming languages, and building projects that help me understand the systems behind their interfaces.

My interests move between software engineering, Linux, automation, electronics and creative work. I enjoy debugging a program just as much as designing a circuit or exploring an unfamiliar tool.
